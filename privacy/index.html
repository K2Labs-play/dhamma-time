Privacy Policy

Dhamma Time

Developer: K2 Labs  |  Package: com.k2labs.dhammatime

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Privacy summary: Dhamma Time does not require an account, does not display third-party advertising, and is designed to keep meditation settings, favorites, listening history, and playback progress on your device. Internet access is used for Dhamma content and related public metadata.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Dhamma Time (the “App”), provided by K2 Labs, accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

2. Information stored on your device

Dhamma Time stores information locally when needed for App functionality. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • meditation timer settings and active timer state;
  • App preferences such as language, theme, timer duration, and bell settings;
  • favorite Dhamma talks;
  • recently played Dhamma talks;
  • audio playback positions used to resume listening;
  • Dhamma catalog metadata cached by the App; and
  • download records and audio files if offline downloading is enabled in a future App release.

This information is stored using Android application storage, including technologies such as Room Database and DataStore. K2 Labs does not maintain a server-side copy of this local App data.

3. Internet access

Dhamma Time uses an internet connection to provide online Dhamma content. The App may connect to Dhamma audio hosting services, archival services such as Internet Archive, Dhamma content websites, and K2 Labs public resources hosted through services such as GitHub or GitHub Pages.

These connections may be used to retrieve catalog information, check for catalog updates, stream Dhamma talks, or download audio when a download feature is available and initiated by you.

Dhamma Time is not designed to send your meditation history, favorites, listening history, or personal profile information to K2 Labs as part of these requests.

Like most internet services, third-party hosting providers may automatically receive standard technical information such as your IP address, request time, and network request details when your device connects to them. Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

4. Dhamma catalog updates

Dhamma Time may check a public K2 Labs catalog endpoint to determine whether newer Dhamma metadata is available. Catalog files may contain Sayadaw names, Dhamma talk titles, collections, content identifiers, audio URLs, durations, and related content metadata.

Catalog update requests are intended to deliver public content metadata and are not intended to identify individual users.

5. Audio streaming

When you choose to listen to an online Dhamma talk, Dhamma Time connects to the relevant audio hosting provider. Audio may continue playing while Dhamma Time is in the background or while your screen is locked. Android media notifications may be displayed to provide playback controls.

K2 Labs does not maintain a server-side listening profile for you.

6. Offline downloads

If a future release enables offline downloads, downloading will be initiated by you and downloaded audio will be stored locally in App-managed storage on your device. Dhamma Time will not upload those downloaded audio files to K2 Labs.

You will remain responsible for the device storage used by downloaded content and will be able to remove downloaded content using App or Android storage controls provided by the relevant release.

7. Meditation timer

The meditation timer operates primarily on your device. The App may use foreground services, notifications, wake locks, vibration, and local meditation bell sounds so sessions can continue reliably while the App is backgrounded or the screen is locked.

Meditation session information is not transmitted to K2 Labs.

8. Notifications

Dhamma Time may request Android notification permission. Notifications may be used for active meditation timers, interval alerts, background audio playback controls, and—if added in a future release—download status or completion.

Dhamma Time does not use notifications for third-party advertising.

9. Permissions and device capabilities

Depending on your Android version and the features you use, Dhamma Time may use capabilities related to internet access, notifications, foreground services, media playback, wake locks, and vibration.

The current App does not require access to your microphone, camera, contacts, phone calls, SMS messages, or precise location for its normal functionality.

10. Accounts and personal information

Dhamma Time currently does not require or provide user registration or account sign-in. K2 Labs does not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, meditation history, or listening history through the App.

11. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current Android project does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs that collect App usage data on behalf of K2 Labs.

Dhamma Time does not display third-party advertisements and does not use the Android Advertising ID for advertising or tracking.

12. Third-party content and services

Dhamma Time provides access to Buddhist Dhamma materials that may be hosted by independent websites, archives, monasteries, publishers, or other content providers. K2 Labs does not control the privacy practices, availability, or infrastructure of those third-party services.

When the App retrieves content directly from a third-party provider, that provider's terms and privacy practices may apply to the network connection.

13. Data sharing

K2 Labs does not sell or rent personal information from Dhamma Time users. Dhamma Time is not designed to share locally stored meditation history, favorites, listening progress, or App preferences with advertisers or data brokers.

Third-party content hosts may receive ordinary technical network information when your device requests content from their servers.

14. Local retention and deletion

Locally stored App information remains on your device until it is changed or removed through the App, cleared through Android app-data controls, or removed by uninstalling the App. App-managed downloaded content, when supported, may also be removed through App or Android storage controls.

15. Security

Dhamma Time relies on Android application sandboxing and permission controls to protect locally stored information. Where supported by the relevant content provider, the App uses HTTPS connections for public metadata and Dhamma audio.

No method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

16. Children's privacy

Dhamma Time does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require an account and is not designed to create advertising profiles or collect behavioral advertising information.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Dhamma Time's features, permissions, catalog infrastructure, content providers, or data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published.
